## Accuracy and completeness
- The AI model can misread photos and markings, misidentify products, or misapply criteria. Results may be incomplete or wrong.
- The bundled recall register is a U.S. starter set reviewed on the date shown in its files. It is not exhaustive and does not cover all recalls, warnings or advisories worldwide.
- Recalls, warnings, remedy programs and manufacturer contacts change. Bundled information can become out of date.
- "No matching recall or replacement warning found" is a bounded search result. It is not proof of safety or proper operation.
- The bundled counterfeit-alert file summarizes selected UL and FM Approvals alerts reviewed on the date shown. It is not exhaustive. A counterfeit alert is not a recall, and the absence of a match does not authenticate a product. Only the issuing body or the manufacturer can confirm a counterfeit.

Verify every result against the original CPSC, manufacturer, UL and FM Approvals notices and with a qualified fire sprinkler professional before making any decision.
