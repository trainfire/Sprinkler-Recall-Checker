## Explicit identification cues
Use archived-identification-facts.json for the historical page-2 temperature tables and page-3 component figure map. Color supports interpretation; it does not establish model, manufacture year, seal construction or recall. In the brochure, blue bulb liquid appears for both 250 F and 286 F. A solder sprinkler's painted frame stripe/deflector dot is different from glass-bulb fluid color. Ordinary temperature numbers must not become date guesses.

The drawings identify specific Star and Gem inscription cues now indexed directly: Gem F927 shows a G-in-triangle logo and SSP-13 deflector inscription; Star SG/SG QR drawings distinguish QR markings; Star concealed Q-QR has QR-Q on the deflector; the ME-1 diagram shows two-digit date locations for its various orientations. SSP, SSP-13, QR or a Star logo alone is insufficient to establish the whole model or a recall match. Locate the exact figure and retain year/construction/scope checks.

The guide's weep-hole depiction can support recognition of the illustrated dry variant when clearly visible with the rest of its geometry. It does not prove O-ring seal construction, and a blurred dark spot is not proof of a hole. Do not ask the user to probe, remove or disturb a sprinkler or cover plate to reveal details. Refer access to a qualified professional. Introductory component Figure F on page 3 depicts an O-ring section; seal-cap Style F on page 9 is a separate classification. Do not interchange the labels.

## Historical program and archive cautions
The adjacent involved.php page resolved to a November 30, 2022 capture and described an Interactive Selector plus downloadable identification brochure. The selector itself was not traversed or validated as an operating tool.
The adjacent index_redo.php page resolved to a June 5, 2023 capture. It explicitly states the August 31, 2007 claim deadline, says no claims will be accepted after that date, and says the announcement adds no new models to the 2001 program. This corroborates the already-bundled CPSC program closure; it does not reopen the program in 2023.

Archived sidebar payment offers, compensation, phone numbers and testing intervals remain historical. Use the current CPSC/Tyco notice for present action and contact context. A 2023 archive date must never be treated as a 2023 manufacture date, brochure revision or newly announced recall. Capture timestamps are provenance, and different followed resources can resolve to different dates.
