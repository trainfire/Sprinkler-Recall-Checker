# Identification rules

## Central O-ring program
Use Tyco's Identification Brochure, pages 5-9, and the candidate figure pages 10-27. Manufacturer logo alone is insufficient. Verify the model against Tables A-F; broad date ranges in announcements are screening aids.
For Central wet commercial/residential/special models in Tables A-C, the guide states that models dated 1997 or earlier use O-ring seals. Starting in 1998, rows marked Yes may use either seal: inspect the model-specific cap using the indicated style A-F on page 9. Rows marked NO* did not undergo that design change; NO* does not mean not recalled. Do not extend the 1998 transition rule blindly to dry models, Gem or Star.
Use Table D for Central dry units. It notes that Belleville replacements are marked TY on the deflector. Verify all inscriptions and manufacturer literature before interpreting TY as a replacement; it is not a universal recall-clearance code.
Distinguish a sprinkler seal cap inside the frame from a ceiling cover plate.

## Other recurring traps
Omega: stacked heat-collector discs are a candidate cue, not conclusive identification of every model. Check the exact notice and inscriptions; Omega and the Central 2001 program are distinct recalls.
Star ME-1: year determines responsible notice. 1961-1976 = Mealane recall; 1977-1982 = AHI/Chemetron recall; 1983-1995 = SCM replacement warning; 1996-1998 = Central program. Do not clear a unit just because it falls outside one notice.
Globe: the recalled Model J is a dry-type series dated 1990-1999. Do not conflate Model J dry with Model JN or other Globe products in the O-ring PDF. Do not infer a dry barrel from the visible head alone if geometry is hidden.
Historical O-ring list: revised November 2009; explicitly not comprehensive. Names/SINs may incorporate O-rings and do not prove recall. Its last-10-years scope and references to NFPA 25-2002 are historical, not current testing rules.
A spare can support identification only after confirming it represents the installed sprinkler being assessed. One unit's result does not establish every unit's status.


## Indexed model and marking checks
The 76-row Tyco index preserves the actual Tables A-F variants, not just a list of model names. Same-name models can have different K-factors, orientations and seal styles: LF pendent K=3.0 uses Style C, while LF horizontal sidewall K=3.5 uses Style A. Wet GB is not dry GB. Resolve the table and variant before applying a seal rule. Use the candidate figure pages listed in the index; an empty figure_pages value means find the figure in the original PDF rather than inventing a page.
Central / CSC inscriptions, Gem's G-in-triangle, and Star / star-shaped logo identify a manufacturer family, not recall applicability. Model markings on the figure can differ from the table's written designation; compare both before treating an abbreviated inscription as a confirmed model. Do not fabricate legacy SINs.
For Tables A-C, NO* and the 1997-and-earlier rule can establish seal construction without seeing the internal seal once model, variant, year and applicable scope are established. Yes rows dated 1998 or later need model-specific cap evidence. Temperature markings such as 155, 165, 200 or 212 are not manufacturing years.
Legacy Star FAQ shows a two-digit date example 74. Accept a two-digit year only where the product's date-marking convention is established; do not apply a general century conversion to every sprinkler. Its wet-sprinkler exclusion is limited to the Mealane dry recall, never blanket clearance of every Star wet model or any sprinkler attached to wet piping. Dry-type sprinklers can connect to wet systems; barrel construction controls.
For Globe dry Model J verify name, J and embossed 1990-1999 year. The current manufacturer advisory page lists ten QR/SR dry configuration drawings; the page title or drawing presence does not independently establish date applicability.

## Source conflicts and field documentation
The Sprinkler Age article conflates the Omega and Central O-ring recalls. Use CPSC: Omega recall October 14, 1998, about 8.4 million since 1982; Central O-ring program July 19, 2001, about 35 million plus limited Gem/Star models. Do not inherit the article's combined model/date/count description.
Association descriptions of the 2003 dynamic O-ring manufacturing change are context, not a recall list or a retroactive compliance determination. Neither O-ring appearance nor manufacturing age alone establishes a specific recall.
Document unit/location, manufacturer/model, date and provenance, decisive construction/variant evidence, uncertain identifiers, notice and verification date, affected quantity if established, written owner notification if made, and replacement completion records. Do not assume one photo establishes an entire building inventory. Do not send owner notifications unless explicitly authorized.

Read archived-identification-facts.json when interpreting bulb fluid, frame markings, introductory component diagrams or a possible dry-sprinkler weep hole. Temperature colors support identification only: the brochure gives blue bulb fluid for both 250 F and 286 F; do not infer a single rating or manufacture year from color. A coating/stripe on a solder sprinkler is different from fluid in a glass bulb.
Figure 59 shows Gem F927 with SSP-13 on the deflector. Figures 61-62 distinguish SG dry standard/quick response through their actual drawings and QR markings; unreadable or unseen QR is not proof it is absent. Figure 64 shows QR-Q for the Q-QR dry concealed variant. These are drawing-specific cues, not universal inscription aliases or independent recall evidence. Figure 60 locates two-digit dates on ME-1 variants; establish the date convention and year before routing to a notice.
The weep-hole cue in these figures is not a universal test for dry construction or O-ring seals. Never probe a sprinkler. ## O-ring construction and corrosion (risk flag)
O-ring seals were the failure mechanism behind the Central, Gem, Star and Omega O-ring actions: CPSC 01-201 states that degradation, corrosion or water contaminants can prevent O-ring sprinklers from activating. Some O-ring models from other manufacturers (for example Globe ESFR-14 / GL1401 on the 2009 list) were never recalled.
- Raise a **Risk flag** when both are true: (1) O-ring construction is indicated by the 2009 list, a Tyco table row, manufacturer literature or the user's own confirmation; and (2) the photo shows corrosion, mineral deposits, verdigris, contamination or leakage at the seal, cap, orifice or frame seat. State that this matches the failure mechanism in the O-ring recalls, that no recall covers this model, and that a qualified sprinkler professional should evaluate it promptly; replacement is the prudent course. Keep the Result line accurate (no recall found does not become a recall).
- O-ring construction without visible corrosion: add one short line in Evidence ("listed as O-ring construction on the 2009 list; not a recall"). Do not raise the flag.
- Corrosion without O-ring information: give a short condition note only.
- The O-ring itself is internal. Describe what is visible ("corrosion around the seal seat"); say the O-ring is confirmed only when the user confirms it or the model is documented as O-ring.

## Edge cases
- **Photo will not open:** say so, give "Unable to determine", ask the user to resend. Do not run lookups or searches.
- **Brand only (STAR, CSC, GLOBE, OMEGA logo; model and year unreadable):** use "Potential recall/warning match" when any of that brand's register records could apply to what is visible, and name them in one line. Use "Unable to determine" only when the manufacturer itself is not established.
- **Removed, loose or spare heads:** the user may rotate and photograph all sides of a head that is already out of service, but must not disassemble it. Never reinstall a removed head. A spare's result applies to installed heads only after correspondence is verified.
- **Mirror-reversed stamps:** front-camera and edited images can show reversed text. Flip before transcribing and say the image appeared mirrored.
- **Catalog or reference images:** screen the pictured head, then ask whether it represents an installed unit.
- **Year uncertain at the 1998 boundary (Tables A-C "Yes" rows):** treat as 1998 or later and require cap evidence.

## Counterfeit and certification alerts
`counterfeit-alerts.json` holds 44 UL and FM Approvals alerts (38 product, 6 certificate), reviewed 2026-10-18 from the original notices. They are not recalls, and they do not implicate authentic products that share a copied designation.
- **Match first, then compare.** A designation or brand match only selects the alert. `external_cues` are issuer-stated differences from authentic units (or self-evident inconsistencies such as a mismatched temperature pair) and one is enough for Suspect counterfeit features. `supporting_cues` (finish, color, font, stamp depth, identifying details) support only a Counterfeit alert lead. `other_markings` are what the issuer printed to identify the counterfeit and serve as lookup keys; genuine units can carry them, so a marking counts only when it also appears in that record's cue lists. A SIN match alone gets no Alert line.
- **Alerts without published cues.** Several UL notices (19PN-25, 19PN-15, 18PN-19, 21PN-22) list only identifying markings. A genuine 2017 TY7226, for example, can match UL 19PN-25 exactly. For these, an Alert line needs a provenance detail, a user concern, or a cue from a related FM alert.
- **Installed units: external cues only.** Typical external cues: bulb markings, deflector markings the authentic model lacks or carries, two brands on one unit, a SIN the brand never used, load-screw tip shape (threaded to the tip versus tapered), cap color and material, compression-screw color, ejection-spring presence, and deflector orientation. Raised versus recessed wrench-boss lettering is a cue only for the Tyco models FM compared (TY3151, TY3251, TY3351; 2013 and 2014 alerts); FM says it is not reliable for Globe. `removed_unit_cues` (inlet, seal, threads, internal parts, magnetism) apply only to a unit already out of service or a verified spare.
- **Keep markings literal.** Do not correct anomalies: 15SSP on the deflector versus Model 15SSSP (UL 19PN-15), GL5601 155°F 168°C (UL 19PN-14), 55° F/68° C on PS007 (UL 14PN-9), K2.5 on TY9226 (UL 25PN-0804).
- **Issuer statements, with their dates and strength.** FM NoC 1164 (2025): a GL5651 dated 2022 or later that is also marked GLOBE can be assumed counterfeit (external). Counterfeit-only bulb markings ATOP⌀5, YDϕ5 / YD05, YH-5 and CDGIX: external on Tyco, Globe or FM-marked units, where FM states the authentic bulbs (Job G5, Geissler G, or Job F3/N3 for GL5601); otherwise a pointer. D77447 (UL 19PN-14) is a supporting cue only, since it may be a copied lot code. Job F5: for the models FM names (Tyco TY3151/TY3251, also UL 14PN-9; Globe GL5651/GL5661/GL5653; Viking VK100) it is a stated difference from the authentic model as of those alerts, with or without an FM mark. On any other FM-marked model it is a lead, because FM (2011 to 2018) says no FM Approved sprinklers use F5 and the 2011 and 2012 alerts say "currently". On other models without an FM mark it is not a cue; genuine Job F5 bulbs exist. FM (2023, 2024): no current FM Approved sprinklers use O-ring seals; this is a removed-unit cue for modern FM-marked units only, since FM notes O-ring seals were in general use until about 2001.
- **Not cues by themselves.** Chrome finish (authentic chrome versions exist for TY5151, TY3351 and others), agency-mark sets (they vary by year on authentic units), white frame paint (temperature class), and finish quality, luster or font on their own. These are supporting cues at most. O-ring construction counts as a counterfeit cue only where a specific alert or issuer statement says so; it never creates a recall, and an alert's O-ring statement does not establish construction for the Risk flag.
- **Model-specific comparisons.** Authentic details differ by model and year. For example, an authentic TY5151 has no TYCO on either wrench flat and the year cast in a frame arm (FM NoC 1142), while authentic TY3151 units in UL 14PN-9 show TYCO on one side and the year on the other. Apply each cue only to the model its alert compares.
- **Concealed sprinklers.** FM Approved concealed bodies are not plated, but the body is hidden by the cover plate. Do not ask for the plate to be removed; ask for records or a verified spare.
- **Location is context.** An alert's country, city or seller is provenance information. It neither confirms nor excludes a unit found elsewhere; several notices say the units may be available in other areas.
- **Certificate alerts.** Six alerts concern falsified FM certificates or registrations (Victaulic V2723/V2703 certificate in the Philippines, Nanjing, Nanjing Tuna, Xiamen Kyrain, Fujian). These are document problems. A sprinkler photo cannot resolve them; route to the FM Approval Guide (approvalguide.com) and FM Approvals.
- **Certification databases.** UL Product iQ and the FM Approval Guide confirm that a legitimate model exists and what it is approved for. They do not authenticate a physical unit, because counterfeits copy real designations.
- **Recall and alert together.** A unit can carry a recall result, a risk flag and an Alert line at once. Report each on its own line.
