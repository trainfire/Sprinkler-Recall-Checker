# Bundled recall and warning summaries

Reviewed 2026-10-13. These are factual summaries, not copies of the full notices and not an exhaustive recall inventory. Follow the exact population and evidence criteria in recall-register.json. A deadline ends the stated benefit program; it does not resolve the hazard in an installed affected sprinkler. Sources and retrieval quality are recorded in sources.md. Do not claim a live check merely because these summaries were read.

## Central O-ring program

CPSC 01-201, July 19, 2001: approximately 35 million Central O-ring sprinklers, plus about 167,000 limited Gem and Star sprinklers. Rubber O-ring degradation, contaminants and corrosion can prevent activation. Central wet table: 48 models dated 1989-2000; Central dry table: 14 models from the mid-1970s to 2001, with the 2026 notice specifying through June 2001; Gem F927 sold under the Gem name 1995-2001; Star dry ME-1, SG, SG-QR, Q and Q-QR dated 1996-1998. Use the register for the complete names and the Tyco index for the actual variant. Manufacturer/model alone can be insufficient because of the seal transition. No population-wide extension to all Central, Gem or Star heads is supported.

Tyco's existing identification guide Tables A-C distinguish Central wet variants: 1997 and earlier use O-rings; models marked NO* had no Belleville design change. From 1998, Yes rows can use either seal and require the correct style comparison. Model names did not always change. Tables D-F have separate dry/Gem/Star rules. A Star-marked F927 is an identification lead from Table E needing scope clarification, not an automatic match to the Star dry recall.

CPSC 03-133, May 28, 2003: modified contractor arrangements and conditional labor reimbursement, rather than a new model population. CPSC 07-169, May 1, 2007: Proof of Claim and Waiver/Release postmark deadline August 31, 2007; the deadline concerned the July 2001 population. These payment and claim procedures are historical.

CPSC release 26-800, September 24, 2026: renewed warning about unreplaced heads, with direction to inspect, replace recalled heads through a qualified professional and review maintenance records. It did not announce a reopened free replacement program. The release cites a July 13, 2025 fire at an assisted living facility in Fall River, MA, where recalled heads identified in an earlier inspection had not been replaced; ten residents died. Rechecked 2026-10-18 with no change. Current Tyco landing page contact, verified October 13, 2026: Central-Sprinkler-O-Ring-Notice@jci.com. The existing bundled FAQ reinforces identification and replacement and notes the 1998 transition. Its mistaken expansion of NFPA must not be repeated. Neither its generalized maintenance language nor the news release substitutes for an adopted NFPA 25 edition.

Sources: [2001 notice](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2001/cpsc-central-sprinkler-company-announce-voluntary-recall-to-replace-o-ring-fire), [2003 modification](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2003/CPSC-Central-Sprinkler-Company-Update-Voluntary-Recall-To-Replace-O-Ring-Fire-Sprinklers), [2007 deadline](https://www.cpsc.gov/Newsroom/News-Releases/2007/August-Deadline-Set-for-End-to-Central-Sprinkler-O-Ring-Sprinkler-RecallProperty-Owners-Should-Act-Now-To-Request-Replacement-of-Sprinkler-Heads), [2026 warning](https://www.cpsc.gov/Newsroom/News-Releases/2026/CPSC-and-Tyco-Fire-Products-LP-Urge-Immediate-Action-on-Recalled-Fire-Sprinklers-that-Can-Fail-in-a-Fire), [Tyco notice](https://www.tyco-fire.com/central-sprinkler-o-ring-notice). ## Omega

CPSC 99-008, October 14, 1998, even though its URL contains /1999/: approximately 8.4 million Omega sprinklers manufactured since 1982. Do not confuse this with the 35-million Central O-ring program. Full model/marking list is in the register. Three spaced, flat metal discs are a useful cue on most models, not sufficient by themselves to confirm identity. The notice reported 30-40% failure in removed test samples, 17 fire failure reports and four injuries; those figures are historical sample/incident data, not a predicted failure rate for an individual sprinkler. CPSC says the recall remedy is no longer available and warns against donation/resale. A qualified professional must arrange replacement of an installed unit; generic discard language is not a DIY removal instruction.

Source: [CPSC Omega notice](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/1999/CPSC-Central-Sprinkler-Recall-Omega-Fire-Sprinklers-Settle-Lawsuit). Full HTML retrieved directly and read October 13, 2026 after the web reader returned 403.

## Star Mealane

CPSC 99-152 displays recall date August 4, 1999. Mealane's legacy outreach letters are dated June 14, 1999; do not substitute an outreach date for the displayed CPSC recall date. Applies to dry-type D-1, RD-1, RE-1, E-1 and ME-1 manufactured 1961-1976, approximately one million units. Verify Star, exact model, manufacture date and dry construction. CPSC reported 67% failure in removed test specimens and one alleged fire failure; do not generalize that sample percentage to every installed head.

The legacy FAQ illustrates a two-digit manufacture date such as 74. Its exclusion of wet sprinklers refers to this Mealane dry recall. It is not universal clearance for all Star products or heads on wet piping. The legacy fact sheet, FAQ, claim and bid pages describe free heads, labor-only bids, manufacturer/model/date/quantity verification, thread-size documentation and later completion verification. Their contact/payment instructions are not verified as a current program; HTML and PDF forms expose different historical addressees. Use current administrator confirmation before giving claim directions. Their safety statements support professional identification and replacement without unscrewing an installed head.

Sources: [CPSC Mealane](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/1999/cpsc-mealane-corp-announce-recall-of-star-fire-sprinklers), [legacy Star home](http://www.star-recall.com/index.htm), [FAQ](https://www.star-recall.com/faq.htm), [fact sheet](https://www.star-recall.com/fact.htm), [bid](https://star-recall.com/bid.htm), [claim](https://www.star-recall.com/claim.htm). ## Star AHI

CPSC 03-116, April 25, 2003: about 60,000 Star ME-1 dry sprinklers manufactured by Chemetron, an inactive AHI subsidiary, 1977-1982. Molded Star, ME-1 and manufacture year identify the affected population. The CPSC page says AHI's reimbursement program concluded and lists No Remedy Available. That does not remove the affected heads' hazard. This is a recall, unlike the separate SCM warning below. Full CPSC page opened 2026-10-14.

Source: [CPSC AHI](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2003/CPSC-American-Household-Inc-Formerly-Sunbeam-Corp-Announce-Recall-of-Star-ME-1-Dry-Fire-Sprinklers-Administrative-Lawsuit-Settlement). Full page opened 2026-10-14; scope also cross-referenced in the SCM notice.

## Star SCM

CPSC 03-117, April 25, 2003: replacement warning for approximately 400,000 Star ME-1 dry sprinklers manufactured by Sprinkler Corporation of Milwaukee 1983-1995. The company had no assets to fund a recall. No free replacement/refund was available; CPSC nevertheless urged immediate replacement. Verify molded Star, ME-1 and manufacture year. Include 1994-1995; historical age/testing language did not exclude those years from the warning. Preserve the label replacement warning instead of treating the unfunded program as no action needed. Full CPSC page opened 2026-10-14.

Source: [CPSC SCM](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2003/CPSC-Warns-Consumers-to-Replace-Star-ME-1-Dry-Fire-Sprinklers-Made-by-Sprinkler-Corporation-of-Milwaukee-Administrative-Lawsuit-Settled-Firm-Financially-Unable-to-Conduct-Recall). Full page opened 2026-10-14 and cross-referenced against AHI.

## Globe Model J

CPSC 07-209, June 12, 2007: about 300,000 Globe Model J Series dry sprinklers manufactured 1990-1999. Pendent, upright and sidewall configurations are included. Globe, J and manufacture year are embossed on the frame. Heads can deteriorate and fail in a fire; five fire failure reports and no injury reports were noted. Sales January 1990-December 1999 do not establish an individual manufacture date. JN and other Globe families on an O-ring list are not equivalent to this Model J dry recall.

CPSC 11-203, April 21, 2011, set a June 1, 2011 postmark deadline for Proof of Claim and Waiver/Release. It did not expand the model population. Do not promise the 2007 $9 head price today. Program closure does not establish that installed units were replaced; verify completion records. Manufacturer advisory drawings can help recognize a configuration but do not prove date applicability.

Sources: [2007 recall](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2007/globe-fire-sprinkler-corp-recalls-dry-fire-sprinklers-model-j-heads-can-fail-to-operate), [2011 deadline](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2011/june-deadline-set-for-end-to-globe-fire-sprinkler-model-j-sprinkler-recall), [manufacturer drawings](https://www.victaulic.com/globe-nfsa-product-advisory/). 2007 full page opened; 2011 full HTML retrieved directly and read October 13, 2026.

## Association context

AFSA / Sprinkler Age, December 9, 2025: recommends reviewing markings and matching notices, using a verified corresponding spare as an identification aid, and documenting discovered recalled equipment for owner action. Floor-level inspection may not expose decisive markings. Its discussion of NFPA 25-2026 4.1.6.1.1 concerns written notification on discovery; verify the adopted standard before giving compliance advice. The article merges different Omega/Central histories; primary CPSC scope controls.

NFSA, November 25, 2025: stresses model/production-specific recall scope, professional replacement rather than repair, and records of locations, quantities, completion dates and contractor. Its discussion of the January 9, 2003 dynamic O-ring manufacturing prohibition is context, not proof that every O-ring sprinkler is recalled or automatically prohibited in existing installations. Use actual recall evidence and adopted standards.

Sources: [AFSA article](https://www.sprinklerage.com/recalled-products/), [NFSA article](https://nfsa.org/2025/11/25/a-quick-peek-above-making-sense-of-sprinkler-recalls/). Both full pages opened October 13, 2026. Summarized selectively; no general ITM compliance assessment is implied.
