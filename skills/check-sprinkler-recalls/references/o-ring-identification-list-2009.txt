Lists of “O”-ring Sealed Sprinklers
The sprinkler models and identification numbers specified herein may incorporate the use
of an O-ring water seal. These lists may not reference all models of O-ring sealed
sprinklers. By examining “spare” sprinklers provided when systems were installed, the
types of sprinklers used in a fire sprinkler system can be identified. If there is a question
as to whether a particular sprinkler model incorporates an O-ring water seal, please
contact the sprinkler manufacturer or UL LLC for assistance related to sprinkler
identification.

The attached List A references “wet” type O-ring sealed sprinklers. List B references
“dry” type O-ring sealed sprinklers. The list of dry sprinklers only includes sprinklers
manufactured within the last 10 years. The current edition of the NFPA Standard for the
Inspection, Testing and Maintenance of Water-Based Fire Protection Systems, NFPA 25-
2002, specifies that dry sprinklers 10 years or older shall be replaced or representative
samples from the system subjected to operational testing. Sprinkler model designations
are provided in bold text and sprinkler identification numbers used more recently are
denoted in plain text.

Please note that these lists may not include those sprinklers that have been recalled.
Information regarding these sprinklers can be accessed at website of the Consumer
Product Safety Commission (CPSC).
